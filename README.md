# NYC Deals

Fixed-price restaurant menus in New York City that are cheap enough to count as Deals: two or more courses for **$45 or less at lunch** or **$75 or less at dinner**, before tax and tip, counting any supplement you can't avoid.

- **Proof.** Every Deal links to the restaurant's own menu. Roundups help find candidates but are never the proof.
- **Freshness.** A Deal drops off if it hasn't been confirmed in 30 days. A weekly job rechecks each menu page, and anything that changed goes on a Recheck List for a human to confirm.
- **Rankings.** Badges from [The Restaurant Report](https://kejjeh.github.io/omakase/), [NYC Eats](https://kejjeh.github.io/nyc-eats/) and [the NYC award roster](https://kejjeh.github.io/nyc-restaurants/) are shown side by side, so cheap can be weighed against good. They are never blended into one score.
- **Restaurant Week.** Menus are included while the program runs, under the same price cap. They come from [nyc-restaurants](https://github.com/Kejjeh/nyc-restaurants).

Site: `docs/` (GitHub Pages). Build: `python scripts/build.py`. Tests: `python -m pytest -q`. See `CLAUDE.md` for everything else.
